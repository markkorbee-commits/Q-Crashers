# MainStage 2026 — canonical build dimensions (creative-director decision)

Authoritative for the stage/crown engineers. Derived from `stage-analysis.md` (photogrammetry on the
official Endshow photo, EXIF 2026-06-27 22:41, K1 box width 1.34 m as scale anchor),
`terrain-analysis.md` (official 2026 floorplan vectors, 2024 aerial) and the storyboard aerials
(f004, f025, f033, f130). World axes: metres, stage deck front edge z = 0, audience +Z, spectator
faces -Z, +X = spectator's right. Tags: FACT / INFERENCE / ASSUMPTION.

Key visual truth: **the 2026 RED is wide but LOW** (INFERENCE, strong): wing tips ~25 m, not 45–50 m.
It reads as a long castle front crowned in the middle by a crouching dragon with two giant bat wings.

  faceted crystal lantern (octahedral) on top, total height ≈ 14.5 m, on fenced plinths (≈5 m square,
  low steel fence) with small cannon props; 2 rows × 4 at x = ±22, z = 46.5 / 73.5 / 100.7 / 127.7
  (FACT floorplan spacing; 2024 aerial agrees on ~44 m row separation). Owned by the world engineer.
* Floor in front of the stage: concrete/brick pavers x ±44, z 0…113 (FACT: AHN + night-test photo),
  grass banks rising to +5.2 m at |x| 100–109.
