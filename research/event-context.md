**Tag legend.** Every claim carries a tag.
- **FACT**: stated by a cited source. Source numbers like [S12] refer to the Sources section. "Frame" means our own storyboard frames (`scratchpad/yt/frames/fNNN_*`, 320x180, one every ~9.88 s), which count as primary visual evidence.
- **INFERENCE**: a strong deduction from facts.
- **ASSUMPTION**: a reasoned fill-in, used where the sources say nothing.
- **UNKNOWN**: not established.

All times are CEST (UTC+2) unless marked UT.

## 1. The 2026 edition

### 1.1 Basic facts
- FACT: The festival was scheduled for 25, 26, 27 and 28 June 2026. Official festival hours were Thu 18:00–23:00 and Fri/Sat/Sun 11:00–23:00. Camping was open from Thu 12:00 to Mon 12:00 [S1].
- FACT: The 2026 edition was sold out [S1]. NOS gives normal capacity as about 72,500 visitors per day [S15]. Omroep Flevoland counts about 55,000 weekend-ticket holders plus about 15,000 day visitors per day [S7][S16]. NL Times says 58,000 campers were expected [S17]. The Q-dance spokesperson says "all 50,000 visitors" received the app message [S8]. INFERENCE: roughly 50–58k people were on site when the festival was cancelled.
- FACT: 2025 drew 268,236 visitors over four days [S18]. Q-dance holds a contract to use the Walibi site until 2032 [S18].

## 2. Theme "Sacred Oath", the dragon, and the RED concept

### 2.1 Official theme texts
- FACT: The theme was revealed at *Defqon.1 The Kick-off* on 2 Oct 2025. It was hosted by Audiofreq, E-Life and Stephanie Magic, and the same event announced D-Sturb as anthem creator [S21][S22].
- FACT (Hard News paraphrasing the reveal): the theme is "a sacred promise that embodies the strength, dedication, and solidarity of the Defqon.1 tribe", "a story that connects the community worldwide" [S21].
- FACT (quoted from the Kick-off by Hardstyle.com): "The Oath is a promise to ourselves to become the best versions of who we are. To love one another and celebrate our dedication to hardstyle. It's all about staying true to yourself and the tribe. We love the individuality of every warrior. For four days on the holy grounds, no matter where you are from, we come together as one tribe, united by our music." [S23]
- FACT (Q-dance anthem release copy): "The most sacred symphony of our tribe has arrived." The copy quotes two anthem lines: "Written in blood and set in stone. / Spoken in truth, we set the tone." [S4]
- FACT (pre-event tagline): "Warriors, the road to glory calls. Join the tribe at Defqon.1 2026 and prepare to return home on June 25, 26, 27 & 28." [S21]
- FACT (third-party guides): "the storytelling, stage design, and overall experience [revolve] around commitment, loyalty, and the unbreakable bond between the community and the music" [S24]. HARD CULTR calls it "the most explicitly ritualistic theme in years" [S25].
- FACT: The in-app gamification layer "The Path" uses the same ritual/tribe vocabulary: Quests, XP, and the ranks Challenger, Warrior, and at rank 3 Berserker, Shaman or Guardian [S26].

### 2.2 The dragon
- FACT: We found no pre-event Q-dance text that names or describes a dragon. The mainstage was meant to stay secret until the festival opened. Omroep Flevoland reports that angry campers marched towards "the main stage that the organisation had wanted to keep secret until the start of the festival" [S27]. Hard News: "The RED-stage was never officially opened, because traditionally it only opens its doors on Friday. As a result, thousands of Weekend Warriors never got to see the imposing structure with their own eyes." [S28]
- INFERENCE: The 2 July Endshow video was the first official public reveal of the 2026 RED design, because the Friday Opening Ceremony, billed as "the first moment to witness this year's stage design" [S19], never took place.
- FACT: Post-event official Q-dance wording about the dragon:
  - YouTube Short title: "The mighty RED took on the shape of a powerful creature, tamed through JDX's Domitor Draconis." [S29]
  - *Sacred Flame* release copy: "One of the most requested tracks since the Defqon.1 2026 Endshow has finally arrived. The emotional melody behind **the weeping dragon** captured the hearts of Warriors around the world." [S30]
- FACT (language): *Domitor Draconis* is Latin for "tamer of the dragon" (*domitor* = tamer, *draconis* = genitive of *draco*).
- FACT (fan community): the stage is called "the Defqon.1 2026 Red stage with the dragon" in fan groups [S31], and "Dragon Cathedral" on TikTok [S32]. A search-engine summary mentioned "petrified dragons", but we found no primary source for it, so it is UNKNOWN or unverified.
- FACT (thumbnail and frames): the design has a giant metallic mechanical dragon head with an open, fanged mouth at centre, spread mechanical wings with gear/rosette discs, and a gothic cathedral/castle facade with towers and pointed arches. Frames #23, #57, #58 and #101 show dragon-head close-ups. Frame #72 shows both wing assemblies engulfed in flame.

### 2.3 Storyline
- FACT: Q-dance's texts only say the RED "took on the shape of a powerful creature", which was "tamed" (Domitor Draconis) and "weeping" (Sacred Flame). The chapter titles are Winter, Sacred Oath, Sacred Flame, Domitor Draconis, Embers and In The Cold [S10][S29][S30].
- ASSUMPTION (narrative arc for the reconstruction):
  1. Frozen, dormant cathedral (Vivaldi "Winter").
  2. Awakening in flames.
  3. The tribe swears the Sacred Oath (anthem).
  4. Ritual guardians tend the Sacred Flame while the dragon weeps.
  5. Darkness, piano, and the dragon awakens and is tamed (Domitor Draconis).
  6. The fire dies down to Embers under the moon.
  7. The cold returns (In The Cold) and the finale fireworks close the show.
  This is a fire-versus-cold arc. It is not confirmed by Q-dance.
- UNKNOWN: whether Q-dance changed the show's story or edit because of the heat cancellation. INFERENCE: the music was all prepared in advance. Tracks were released on 3/10 July and 17 July, and "In The Cold" carries the subtitle "(The Story)" [S33].

### 2.4 RED (mainstage) concept, official
- FACT (Q-dance): "our mighty mainstage known as the RED", "the undisputed center of the Holy Grounds". The Saturday Endshow is "the biggest show spectacle within the harder styles … pushes boundaries every year with record-breaking amounts of pyro, brand-new hardstyle tracks, innovative show techniques, and special acts" [S19].
- FACT (Q-dance): an Exclusive RED Experience deck with a "luxurious tent with a large sky terrace right in front of the RED" [S19]. INFERENCE: a raised VIP deck sits on the field in front of RED. See the empty-field frames. The deck is not identified in them.
- FACT: HARD CULTR reports that in 2026 "GPF officially takes over the RED stage" (Sunday) [S25].

## 4. How the Endshow was filmed

### 4.1 Date: Saturday 27 June 2026 (evening)
Evidence:
1. FACT: Omroep Flevoland: "The festival site was closed on Saturday evening. Q-dance says that on Saturday evening only the authorities involved and professionals were present." [S9]
2. FACT: Hard News: "rumours were already cautiously starting among fans on Saturday evening. Warriors still staying at the surrounding holiday parks reported hearing fireworks from the festival site." [S28]
3. FACT: EDMTunes (29 June): "pyrotechnics were indeed fired, and the sky over Biddinghuizen briefly lit up with lasers" on Saturday night [S52]. EDMTunes (29 June) also cites the Reddit post "Fireworks at Defqon.1 right now" by u/svenikip [S53].
4. FACT: Q-dance: "on the empty Holy Grounds, we brought The Endshow to life and captured every moment" [S11][S54]. Q-dance via ANP: "the complete production, including live acts, special effects and fireworks, was still performed and recorded" [S55].
5. INFERENCE: The moon is visible in frame #113 (video 18:36) as a nearly full disc, low above a lantern pillar. On 27 June 2026 the moon was 96% lit (waxing gibbous; full moon 29 June 23:57 UT). It rose at 20:46 CEST at az ~137° and stood 7.7–8.6° high at az 163–169° between 22:40 and 23:06 (ephem computation). That fits 27 June but does not rule out 26 or 28 June.

### 4.2 Why a show with no audience
- FACT (Q-dance spokesperson): there was permission to hold the Endshow without an audience, and there was "urgent advice" to set off the fireworks: "It is safer to set this off than to dismantle, pack and transport it." This happened within the permit conditions [S9].
- FACT (Mayor Gebben): "It would take two months to take the fireworks apart … Compare it to putting the pin back into a hand grenade. It was safer to let the fireworks go off." [S39]
- FACT (Q-dance): "Some moments are too sacred to remain unseen. After everything that happened last weekend, we knew we couldn't leave this chapter unfinished." [S11][S54] Also: "Nothing can replace experiencing The Endshow on the Holy Grounds of Defqon.1 with more than 70,000 Warriors. But after everything that happened last weekend, we wanted to give the Tribe one more moment to come together. Tonight is not about what we missed, but about celebrating everything we built together and what connects us." (Sander Bijlstra) [S56]

### 4.3 Time of day
- FACT: The official slot was 22:40 on Saturday [S2]. Sunset in Biddinghuizen on 27 June was 22:03:53 CEST, civil twilight ended at 22:53:38, and nautical twilight ended at 00:14:53. The sun never gets below −14.3° (at 01:41), so there is no astronomical darkness. These values come from our ephem computation for 52.44 N, 5.666 E.
- INFERENCE: The video sky gets steadily darker. Median sky RGB in wide and aerial shots is about #002C5A–#002E67 (deep twilight blue with a teal-green horizon glow) at 00:00–02:30. By ~10:00–10:30 it is about #00001A, and from ~20:00 onward it is near-black. That matches a single run starting near the 22:40 slot (sun −4.7°) and ending about 23:06 (sun −7.1°). Camera exposure and grading are unknown, so treat the start time as **22:40 ± 20 min (INFERENCE)**.
- FACT (ephem): sky objects during 22:40–23:06. Twilight glow is centred at sun az 319°–325° (NW). Venus (mag −4.0) stood at alt 12.7°→8.9°, az 282°–287°, and Jupiter (mag −1.7) at alt 4.9°→1.5°, az 298°–303°. The moon is given in §4.1.
- INFERENCE: The bright point high over the stage in the aerial frames #3–#6 is most likely Venus or a drone light. UNKNOWN which.

## 7. The Defqon.1 show-moment tradition

### 7.1 Structure of a Defqon.1 weekend
FACT [S2][S19][S18][S69]:
- **Thursday: The Gathering.** "Weekend Warriors, Welcome Home" opening words [S69]. Also The Tribute (UV).
- **Friday: The Opening Ceremony.** A "symphonic show and live experience" at RED, and the first look at the new stage design. Later: RED Race, and **The Spotlight**, a 30–40 min artist-led endshow.
- **Saturday: Warrior Workout, POWER HOUR, The Endshow.** POWER HOUR has existed since 2014, lasts 60 minutes, and features confetti, inflatables and the LEFT-RIGHT moment. The Endshow is "the biggest show spectacle within the harder styles".
- **Sunday: The Closing Ceremony**, extended from 3 to 5 hours in 2024, with Defqon.1 Legends. It ends with **The Closing Ritual**, "the awe-inspiring endshow experience on Sunday" [S19][S70].

### 7.3 Typical Endshow structure
- FACT (2025 example [S73]): cinematic intro (0–2:00), then about six new or exclusive tracks, then the anthem (Vertile, *Where Legends Rise*, at 15:50–20:13 of 26:39, ~59% in), then an emotional vocal finale (Sound Rush & Sogma ft. Ekaterina Shelehova, *Stay With Me*) with the fireworks climax.
- FACT (EDMTunes description of the tradition): "exclusive music premieres, massive pyrotechnics, synchronized lasers, towering flames, and cinematic storytelling" for "more than 60,000 Weekend Warriors" [S53].
- INFERENCE: 2026 follows the same pattern (orchestral/cinematic intro, story tracks, emotional vocal finale with the biggest fireworks) with one unusual choice: the anthem comes early, at 4:34 (17%). There is also a comic "breather" (L.P.A.) before the dramatic core.

### 7.4 Rituals and vocabulary
- FACT: "**Forever One Tribe.**" is Q-dance's sign-off, used on the cancellation update page, the Endshow description, and the premiere framing [S42][S11][S56]. "One Tribe" was the 2019 theme [S18]. The community is "the Tribe", visitors are "Warriors" or "Weekend Warriors", and the grounds are the "Holy Grounds" [S1][S19].
- FACT: Flags are part of the fabric of the grounds. Rioters pulled "flags out of masts" [S27], so flag masts line the site. Q-dance merchandise describes the red Defqon.1 flag as a symbol of loyalty to the tribe; this is from a search-result snippet of store.q-dance.com [S74], so it is a weaker FACT. Visitors come from 100+ countries [S18]. INFERENCE: crowds normally fill the field with national and Defqon.1 flags. In 2026 the field was empty.
- FACT: Q-dance's emotional colour is orange: "a proud orange heart" and the 🧡 emoji [S11][S75]. The stage brand colour is RED.

## 8. Aftermath (brief)
- FACT: Refund or keep-for-2027 choice with a 31 July deadline. Refunds ran from mid-August to mid-September, and travel and add-ons were refunded automatically [S42]. More than 75% of buyers kept their tickets, which leaves fewer than 25% of 2027 tickets for the late-October sale [S48].
- FACT: Q-dance is insured, according to the spokesperson [S8][S47]. Stranded visitors were housed at a Biddinghuizen holiday park [S8].
- FACT: Community events filled the gap: Free Bootshaus Rave in Cologne, a free event in Amsterdam from MC Villain, Korsakoff and MeWe, "FOR THE CULTURE" in Utrecht, DWAAS in Breda, and livestreams from Da Tweekaz, The Smiler and Sound Rush [S45].
- FACT: Hardstyle DJ Outsiders condemned the rioters: "please never come back. You are not one of us." [S50]
