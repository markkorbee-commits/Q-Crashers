## What changed in `spans/01.json` (594 cues, 76 camera shots; previously 216 and 23)

  from the list). The framing families are: far aisle (0, 4, 146) tilted up at fov 40; telephoto wide / telephoto /
  tight from the same riser; a low side camera; dragon close-ups; deck-side; aerial; the vocal drone pull-back
  (moving); rear drones (moving); a side drone; a lantern close-up; and a stage drone. All shots pass the validator's
  sight lines.
* **110–114:** the wing outline now fades with the 112.07 silence (it used to stay lit until 118.6). I removed the
  invented sky beam (110.9) and strobe hits (114.9, 121.0).
* **Lantern crystals (114.5–133.3):** a phrase-by-phrase blue → cyan → teal drift, off in the gaps, following the
  audio onsets. **Warm set flashes** on 120.70 and 131.0, then the white look (131.4), then dark with blue posts.
* **Laser web:** kept, but the lanterns are now **off** during it (they used to pulse cyan). The stage returns at 143.5
  with white sky fans behind the wings.
* **Heavy kick:** per-beat look changes follow the montage. There are **two burning-wing firewalls** (148.03 and
  154.14, 16 beats apart) and **four CO2 blasts** (145.93, 146.31, 148.79, 149.37). Beam fans play only where the video
  shows them. I removed the 12 s strobe kick.
* **157.8–160.8:** the section kind changed from silence to **breakdown**. The video shows lit looks there, not a
  blackout.
* **160.8–171.5:** a colour chase on every beat (lavender, cyan, green, pink, full, white, garlands, …) on the LEDs,
  lanterns, shafts and wash. I added blue- and green-lit CO2 and a centre CO2. I removed the invented green laser sheet
  (166.9–176.1); no green sheet appears anywhere in 160–206.
* **Riser:** dark, blue side beams, then warm garland light in orange fog.
* **Vocal:** a red/blue crown throughout, with the magenta flash at 195.99–196.56 (it used to be magenta from 183.8 to
  195.2, then dim red), dimmer from 197.5. I added blue floor pools and white side beams, then the violet deck fans,
  the blue laser fans (201.5–203.4), and dark until the 206.3 impact.
* **Liquid sky:** now starts on the 206.295 impact, not the section boundary. The sky fans are lower and fainter, and
  the vertical `sky` beams were replaced by side fans.
* **Peacock fan:** this used to be 2 gold comet volleys and 1 roof fan. It is now a comet fan from the dragon crest
  every beat from 218.92, growing from 1 gold comet to a 160° silver semicircle of 12 comets (58 m), until 231.5. I
  removed the pink/gold tilt-wave beams and the 2-bar light hits.
* **Pre-drop:** no longer a blackout (it used to have stage master 0 from 233.8 to 243.4). The set stays red/blue, and
  I added side beams (234.59), amber floor beams, a CO2 puff, beams onto the deck, the white-out strobe burst
  (238.22–239.37), beam fans, a static stage, the blue laser fan (242.68) and the white look (243.0).
* **Drop:** the pyro is rebuilt from the video. Crossing white-gold comets on the deck every 2 beats plus vertical
  white comets, silver roof comets, gold side-section comets every beat, and corner fountain-fans (243.4–249.5). Then
  white corner fans and small deck comets (249.5–252.55). Then the green/white comet wall across the U (252.55–254.1).
  Then wing-tip V-fans with crackle every half beat (255.61–264.78). Then a white fountain wall (264.78), white comets
