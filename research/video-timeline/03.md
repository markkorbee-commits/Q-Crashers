## Not expressible yet

* **Pearl heads on comet rows:** `end: 'pearl'` renders as two huge bloom blobs wherever several arm comets
  line up with the lens, so the pearls were dropped.
* **Bengal smoke position:** `pyro.bengal` is not in the validator's vocabulary, so the red Bengal smoke uses
  `pyro.burst` with dur ≥ 2 s.
* **Per-zone LED colour:** there is no way to colour the wings red and the side sections/castle blue at the same
  time (break and final kick). `screens.content` colours the whole structure.
* **MC after v498:** the MC performer's window ends at 498 s, but the video shows the MC on the deck until v502.
  The shots at 498.3–502 show an empty deck.
* **Short laser segments:** the lavender low laser fans in the quiet sections read in the video as short V
  segments at the deck. Our beams run the full length through the haze, so they are dimmed as a compromise.
* **Crackle comets:** crackle comets always break into crackle shells, so the canopy's crackling fan tails are
  approximated with shells. The `crackle` pops branch in `cometFan` is unreachable, because `crackle` is a
  `SHELLS` key.
