## Known gaps

* The real piano sits on a ~5 m scaffold tower; ours is a 0.9 m riser, so the tower telephotos and the CO2 jets
  (which start on the tower top in the video) cannot match; the pianist close-up composition differs with it.
* Lantern crystals cannot be lit one at a time; the dragon "breathing" is approximated by stage master + wash;
  the dragon is lit by its LED lines, the filmed head is flood-lit red / green (much brighter at v947, v1008, v1044).
* Our crown (wings ~12 % larger, head higher) does not fit all filmed reference points at once, so some telephoto
  poses were chosen by the metric rather than by the fit.
* `trees` tents from the deck do not read from 170 m at height 6–18; the filmed Λ tents of the laser section are
  drawn as the tall `grid` lattice.
* `pyro.waterfall` has no density / height parameter: the gold glitter rain at v1092.3 is much denser and taller.
* A zoom inside a long shot needs the shot split into static / moving parts (v998.4, v1051.6).
* The exact piano-bounce topology (first beam off L1, then R1 …) differs from the engine's fixed order.
