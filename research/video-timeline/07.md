# Video timeline — span 07: JDX – Domitor Draconis, 886.08–1098.37 (show s)

Source: official YouTube edit, frames at 4 fps (scratchpad `vid/f4`), signals `vid/signals/07.txt`.
Show time = video − 0.036. The track has no stable tempo (audio map: `dom_free` 881.6–1030.1 and `dom_free2`
1066–1097.4 are free, `dom_100` 1030.1–1066 has a 0.600 s percussion period, nominal anchor 1032.042). Cues are
therefore placed on measured `onsets[]` (±0.15 s) or, where no onset is near, on the visual time; the flame
bursts of 1032–1041 sit on the 0.6 s grid / its onsets. The measured cut list missed ten cuts in dark footage
(v961.7, v967.0, v1020.0, v1022.45, v1036.75, v1037.75, v1051.25, v1074.7, v1080.85); they were found by frame
correlation and checked by eye. The strobe "cuts" v1011.4–1014.3 are flashes, not cuts.

The previous (storyboard-based) authoring of this chapter differed from the video in most places: there is no
blackout with blue deck fans at 1003.5, the green/amber "climax 1" look appears at 1023.3 (not 1006.8), the
fire section is 1031.7–1041.9 (not "twin torches" from 1027.6), 1046–1049 is lit violet (not a blackout),

## Known gaps

* The real piano sits on a ~5 m scaffold tower; ours is a 0.9 m riser, so the telephoto shots over the tower
  cannot match its composition; the real telephotos (≈ 5° fov) are narrower than the show camera allows (10°).
* Lantern crystals cannot be lit one at a time; the dragon "breathing" is approximated by stage master + wash
  cross-fades; the castle LED battens stay green where the video shows a dark castle under a lit dragon.
* The exact piano-bounce topology (first beam off L1, then R1…) differs from the engine's fixed BOUNCE order.
* f104 storyboard warning (pyro 1030.6–1032.6): the video has no fire before 1031.7 (frontal shot 1026.9–1031.8
  shows none), so the gap 1030.6–1031.7 is correct.
