## Open points / engine requests

- **Flare drones:** airborne moving flares are approximated by static Bengals.
- **Capital flames:** the video has two tongues at the capital corners beside the crystal. The engine fires one column per anchor, so they sit above the crystal (`pillars_top`, angle 20).
- **Warm festoon bulbs:** these are strings along the wing tops and side sections. They are approximated by turning the LED lines warm. The engine has no cue for bulb strings.
- **The laser sunburst (377–380):** rings and spirograph circles. The `tunnel` and `cone` presets only approximate them.
- **Comet columns:** the video has thin tails with bright heads. Ours have heavy tails that read as solid lines when a volley fires on every beat.
- **Set proportions:** the video's wings are much larger than our model, so the telephoto framings show more of the side sections.
