## 1. [blocker] Pyro, gerb and firework cores never reach white (module postfx, lens: ENDSHOW RECONSTRUCTION ACCURAC)
In the video, fire cores burn white (overexposed) with coloured falloff. In ours they stay saturated red, salmon or orange everywhere, so every climax looks flat and 'painted'. Default 64 moments: mean share of near-white pixels (R, G and B all > 200) is 0.59 % in the video vs 0.02 % in ours. Of the 8 moments where the video has more than 1 % white, ours goes above 0.5 % once. Finale: v1520 10.19 % vs 0.01 %, v1525 4.44 vs 0.00, v1527.5 7.61 vs 0.00, v1530 6.76 vs 0.00, v1511.75 4.3 vs 0.0. Eruption v1565.75: 21.3 % vs 0.01 %. Anthem v1509.25: 18.2 vs 0.0. Beam storm v1243: 4.6 vs 0.0. Mean saturation is also lower: 0.57 vs 0.74 in the video. Quality medium, Show camera, exposure 0.5.
Evidence: work/sim/qa12_fin/pairs.json (ours_06080/06100/06120/06263.png vs f4 06080, 06100, 06120, 06263); work/sim/qa12_accuracy (all 64 pairs); work/sim/qa12_worst_b.jpg (v1511.75 f4 06047, v1536.25 f4 06145); work/compare/qa12_t8c.jpg (v1520 f4 06080: video white fan over a magenta dragon, ours a uniform red frame)
Suggested fix: Keep SHOWCAM_EXPOSURE at 0.5 (src/camera/CameraRig.ts). Instead give spark heads, gerb columns and comet heads an HDR core 4–8x the current emissive (src/pyro, src/fireworks). Or lower uTone2.x/y in the tonemap() path-to-white (src/postfx/shaders.ts) so pixels above about 0.8 of display peak blend to white. Target: at least 3 % white pixels at v1520/1525/1530 and at least 10 % at v1565.75, without raising the luma of the dark field.

## 7. [major] FxLayer draws whole 32-particle slots: 50-55 % degenerate instances, the ultra triangle budget broken at firework/pyro peaks, spark pool saturated (module fireworks, lens: Technical quality, performance)
src/fx/core/FxLayer.ts sets this.geo.instanceCount = slot * S with slotSize 32 (FireworkSystem.ts:170 fw-stars, FxShared.ts:163 pyro sparks and ribbons). Every emitter occupies ceil(eff/32) slots, and the unused tail of each slot is still instanced (vertex-shaded, then collapsed). Measured at ultra, spot=middle: t=270 fw-stars 3,608 slots = 115,456 instances for 52,008 particles (55 % padding, 1.39M triangles from this one draw). t=1536: fw-stars 2,835 slots = 90,720 instances for 44,374 particles; pyro-sparks 4,368 slots = 139,776 instances for 69,430 particles, with the pool at 4,368/4,375 (saturated; high shows 3,291/3,282). Result: ultra exceeds the desktop triangle bound (≤ 3M, the shot.mjs --budget rule for all non-mobile presets). spot=middle sweep (every 2 s): 15 of 791 moments over 3M (254: 3.53M, 270: 3.77M, 786: 3.25M, 788: 3.04M, 1526-1538: 3.16-4.03M, 1566-1570: 3.20-3.35M). The Show camera sweep adds 788 (3.20M), 878 (3.04M) and 1536 (3.03M), and ultra playback hit 3.16-3.70M across 1526-1534. HIGH stays within the documented HIGH budget (max 2.97M middle, 2.41M Show camera). Draw calls are fine everywhere (max 130, overview pose). Mobile passes budget-check.mjs but has 1 % headroom: 791k/800k at t=268 (fw-stars again). The other large constant is the crowd: 1.37M triangles at ultra (hero 150×2786 = 418k, near 754×698 = 526k, mid 3996×107 = 427k).
Evidence: Triangle attribution by wrapping renderBufferDirect (attrib.mjs): t=1536 fx:fw-stars 1,088,640, fx:pyro-sparks 559,104, crowd-near 526,292, crowd-mid 427,572, crowd-hero 417,900; t=270 fx:fw-stars 1,385,472. System stats (shot.mjs): t=270 'fw-stars.particles':52008, 'fw-stars.slots':'3608/7313'; t=1536 'pyro-sparks.particles':69430, 'slots':'4368/4375'. Sweeps: ultra middle tris>3M at 15/791 moments, max 4,027,215 @1536; ultra showcam max 3,196,661 @788; high middle max 2,973,655; high showcam max 2,411,197; budget-check.mjs mobile PASS (1536 overview 105/110 calls). Renders .shots/qa12-fw-270.png, .shots/qa12-fw-1536.png; video f4/01080.jpg, f4/06144.jpg.
Suggested fix: Make the instance count exact. Keep per-slot (row, offset, count) but pack slots so each slot's count is its real particle count, and upload a prefix-sum/offset texture so instance i maps to (slot, local index). Then instanceCount = sum(eff). Alternatively use slotSize 8-16 for ribbon layers (stars, sparks), where the padding cost is (segments+3)*2 vertices per wasted instance. This brings ultra to about 3.0M at 1536, frees spark slots at the finale, and gives mobile real headroom at t=268.
