## 1. [major] The 2026 heat story does not work in the crowd: density read at the player's empty cell, 'feels 23 °C' in a 3 p/m² crowd, hyperthermia and collapse cannot be reached (module perception, lens: Educational intoxication simul)
PerceptionSystem.estimateActivity uses crowd.densityAt(p.x, p.z) at the player's exact 2 m grid cell. The crowd layout leaves that cell empty at every named spot. Measured at spot=crowd (6, 32): max density within r = 0-1 m = 0.00 p/m², r = 2: 0.88, r = 4: 2.38, r = 6: 3.44. So packed = 0, microC = 0 and exposure = 1 (full 4.5 m/s breeze). The status line reads 'Air 22.5 °C · 81 % humidity · feels 23 °C · Dancing' in the front crowd (spot crowd and spot middle, stats feels 22.6). This contradicts the panel's own note that a dense crowd 'blocks the breeze and adds its own heat'. It also contradicts bible §12.3 (+3 °C in zone A, +1.5 °C in zone B, wind blocked). Real-time XTC run at spot=crowd, activity forced to Dance, 330 s: temperature 37.2 → 38.5 at t≈135 s ('Heat danger' from 150 s) → peak 39.0 °C at 225-255 s. HYPERTHERMIA_C 39.5 and COLLAPSE_C 40 are never reached, so on the default Endshow night the key 2026 lesson ('You are overheating' card, 'You collapsed' outcome) never fires from a spot. The widget also shows 'feels 23 °C' next to a red 38.7 °C 'Heat danger: … walk out of the crowd' line.
Evidence: qa12-xtc-run-180.png (38.7 °C heat danger next to 'feels 23 °C'), qa12-xtc-run-90.png. XTC run log t150-t255: temp 38.6-39.0, feels 22.6-23.4. Density ring log at (6, 32): '0:0.00, 1:0.00, 2:0.88, 3:1.75, 4:2.38, 6:3.44'. The tier runs at spot=middle all report feels 22.6 / Dancing 0.64. Show t 560-900 (f4 02240-03600).
Suggested fix: In estimateActivity, sample the density over a neighbourhood, e.g. the max or a 1/r-weighted mean of densityAt at 8 directions × radii 2, 4, 6 m. Alternatively, build the density grid before the spot clearings are applied. Then re-tune so that XTC plus Dance in zone A reaches 39.5 °C late in the plateau, while resting or walking out of the crowd visibly brings it down. Add a unit test: densityAt-based packed > 0.5 at spot 'crowd'.

## 2. [major] Alcohol 'contrast' lifts night blacks to grey: every tier looks like a washed-out video, not impaired vision (module postfx, lens: Educational intoxication simul)
The composite shader's contrast stage (shaders.ts ~l.655-659) mixes 20 % of a linear pivot around mid-grey 0.18 into the log-space contrast: col = mix(lc, mix(vec3(0.18), col, c), 0.2). In display space this adds 0.2·0.18·(1-c) linear light to every pixel, which sRGB encoding turns into a large black-floor lift on a night scene. Same camera, same show moment (spot=middle, t 1230), full-frame 8-bit luma. Sky mean / frame p50 / frame p1: sober 1.5 / 2.0 / 0.0; 0.5‰ 12.4 / 14.0 / 7.2; 0.8‰ 16.4 / 18.0 / 11.0; 1.2‰ 21.3 / 23.2 / 11.2; 1.6‰ 24.8 / 26.9 / 12.1. At 0.32‰ ('first effects') a grey lift is already visible on the paving. The realistic preset has the same table (0.5‰ → contrast 0.91), so it is affected too (qa12-cmp-realistic-08-416.png: the paving and fence on the altered half turn grey). Alcohol lowers contrast sensitivity, glare recovery and peripheral vision (bible §12.2 '-10 % contrast'). It does not turn a black night sky dark grey. The veil is the dominant visible effect at 0.5‰ and makes the sim look like a cheap filter.
Evidence: qa12-bac-0-1230.png, qa12-bac-05-1230.png, qa12-bac-08-1230.png, qa12-bac-12-1230.png, qa12-bac-16-1230.png (show t 1230, f4 04920). Compare splits qa12-cmp-strong-05-416.png, qa12-cmp-strong-12-416.png, qa12-cmp-realistic-08-416.png (t 416, f4 01664). qa12-toast-45s.png (0.32‰).
Suggested fix: Drop the flat 0.18 veil term, or scale it by the scene's adapted luminance (the bloom mip average or the exposure key, about 0.02-0.05 at night) so a dark scene keeps its blacks. Put the contrast loss into the log-space pow around the adapted mean, plus glare-dependent veiling (veil ∝ local bright surroundings, as the XTC veil already does with veilGate). Target: at 0.5‰ sky mean ≤ 3/255 and p1 ≈ 0; at 1.6‰ ≤ 6/255.

## 3. [major] The XTC plateau reads as rewarding (brighter, warmer, more saturated, beat-synced pulses), against bible §12.1 'never rewarding' (module perception, lens: Educational intoxication simul)
The default 'strong' XTC gains add sat +0.4, warmth 0.85, glow 0.35, bloom 0.9 and star streaks 0.55. They also add beat-synced 'kick' pulses (kickSat 0.08, kickZoom 0.008, kickBloom 0.6, kickExp 0.08), so the show looks more intense on every kick. In the compare split at the beam storm (t≈1231), the altered half is +23 % brighter (mean luma 31.6 → 38.8) and +40 % more chromatic (13.4 → 18.7), with teal halos: an 'Instagram' look. At the anthem-drop red cue (run t≈90 s), the whole frame, sky and paving included, floods saturated red-magenta. At t≈180 s it becomes a milky pink-white glow field. Discomfort cues (nausea tint, jaw, nystagmus) are weak by comparison and only appear in the onset. Beat-synced enhancement of the show is the clearest 'reward' signal and has no physiological basis. The comedown (xtcTime 235) is barely distinguishable from sober apart from mild fringes.
Evidence: qa12-xtc-compare.png (t 1231, f4 04924), qa12-xtc-plateau-416.png (t 416, f4 01664), qa12-xtc-run-90.png and qa12-xtc-run-180.png (spot crowd, t≈650-740), qa12-xtc-comedown.png. XTC_GAINS.strong in src/intoxication/models.ts.
Suggested fix: Remove kickSat, kickZoom and kickBloom (or tie them to discomfort: a brief blur or dim on the kick instead of a boost). Cut warmth and glow and keep dilated-pupil dazzle as glare that costs detail: veil gated by bright surroundings, loss of contrast in the dark areas. Make the discomfort layer persistent through the plateau: nystagmus-driven blur bursts, jaw-clench shake, a periodic nausea tint and a tunnel that grows with body temperature. Show the comedown clearly (dSat, dContrast and dExp are too small to read). Check with the compare split that the altered half is not brighter or more saturated than sober at t 416 and 1231.

## 4. [major] At '2.00‰ DANGER' the risk line says 'over the Dutch driving limit'. Raw and rounded BAC disagree, so the poisoning warning never shows from the 2.0 preview (module ui, lens: Educational intoxication simul)
The widget and panel round BAC to 2 decimals (PerceptionUI.update) to choose the tier and the red 'danger' state. RiskModel.collectWarnings uses the raw value. After the 2.0‰ preview button, elimination immediately drops BAC to 1.9996, so the widget shows '2.00‰ · DANGER · 2‰+' in red with the Find-first-aid button, but the only warning is 'Above 0.5‰: over the Dutch legal driving limit.' (drivingLimit, bac < 2 branch). The alcoholDanger text ('risk of alcohol poisoning… call 112') never appears. The same class of mismatch occurs at 0.50‰, where the tier says 'Over the driving limit' but warnings = 0 (raw 0.496). About 20 s later the panel shows 'VERY DRUNK · 1.6-2‰' while the '2.0‰' preview chip stays highlighted.
Evidence: qa12-firstaid.png ('2.00‰ DANGER · 2‰+' plus the driving-limit line). qa12-alc-panel-desktop.png (1.99 'Very drunk' with the 2.0‰ chip on). Tier-run stats: bac0.5 warnings 0; status0.5 has no warning line.
Suggested fix: Pass the displayed BAC (Math.round(bac·100)/100) into RiskInput, or give the thresholds a small hysteresis (enter danger at ≥ 1.995, leave at < 1.9). Make previewBac hold the level briefly: pause elimination for about 20 s after a preview, or set 2.0 + ε.

## 5. [major] The live alcohol widget is thin on education between 0.5 and 2.0‰: the only risk line is the driving limit, with no festival or heat risk (module ui, lens: Educational intoxication simul)
From 0.5 to 1.99‰ the status widget shows only 'Above 0.5‰: over the Dutch legal driving limit.' at 0.8, 1.2 and 1.6‰ ('Very drunk'). That is irrelevant at a festival. The festival-relevant consequences (falls and crowd risk, vomiting, memory gaps, losing friends) and the 2026 heat message (alcoholHeat: 'Alcohol + heat dehydrates…') do not appear. alcoholHeat is gated on hydration < 0.9 or the heatwave scenario, and alcohol-only sweating keeps hydration at 0.98-0.99 during normal play. The tier effects appear only in the X panel and in a 9 s tier toast after a real drink.
Evidence: Tier runs: status0.8, status1.2 and status1.6 all read '…Above 0.5‰: over the Dutch legal driving limit.' (qa12-bac-08-1230.png, qa12-bac-16-1230.png; stats hydration 0.98-0.99).
Suggested fix: Add one tier-specific live line per tier, rotating with the existing sub-line. For example, 0.8: 'Glare lingers and balance drops — watch your step on the steps and banks'. 1.2: 'Falls and getting lost in the crowd: stay with friends, alternate with free water'. 1.6: 'Memory gaps and vomiting — never walk off alone'. Show alcoholHeat from 0.5‰ on the Endshow night too (humid 22.5 °C night after a 31 °C day). Keep the driving-limit line as a secondary rotating line.

## 6. [major] 'Resting · stand up' button appears by itself and does nothing when clicked (module ui, lens: Educational intoxication simul)
With any alcohol and the player standing still for 5 s, auto-rest sets riskInput.resting. The status widget then shows the action button 'RESTING · STAND UP' although the player never chose to rest (seen at the bar and on mobile). Clicking it calls setResting(false) → activityMode 'auto', which immediately auto-rests again. The toast says 'Back on your feet' but the label stays 'RESTING · STAND UP' and activity stays 'Resting 0.08' (two clicks tested).
Evidence: qa12-rest-button.png, qa12-m-bac12.png (mobile, 1.2‰ at bar_west). Log: before/after-click1/after-click2 all {label 'RESTING · STAND UP', resting true, act 'Resting 0.08'}.
Suggested fix: Drive the button from the explicit activityMode ('rest' selected by the user), not from auto-rest. When only auto-resting, hide the rest button in alcohol mode or show 'Rest & cool down'. Show the automatic state in the air/activity line only.
