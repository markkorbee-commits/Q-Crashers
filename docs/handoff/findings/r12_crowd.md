## 1. [major] Fire lanterns render as white unlit cubes (module performers, lens: ENDSHOW RECONSTRUCTION ACCURAC)
During the fire ritual (v649–728) the troupe carries lanterns that glow warm orange with fire in the video; they are the light source of these close-ups. Ours are white or grey box cubes with no emissive glow and no light on the hands or faces.
Evidence: work/compare/qa12_t5a.jpg rows 1–3 and 5 (v650 f4 02600, v690 f4 02760, v709.25 f4 02837, v725 f4 02900); qa12_worst_b.jpg row 5 (v705 f4 02820)
Suggested fix: Give the lantern mesh a warm emissive core (#FFB050, HDR 3–5) with a flicker that is a pure function of show time, plus a small additive glow sprite. Optionally add a cheap light that lights the carrier's hands and face in close-ups.

## 2. [blocker] Start spots have a large empty foreground (clear-view cone): 'In the crowd' looks like a parade ground (module crowd, lens: Crowd, performers & immersion )

- spot 'middle' (the DEFAULT start spot): 0.53 p/m² at 6–9 m, 0.83 at 9–12 m, 1.0 at 12–15 m and 1.49 at 15–20 m, against the zone-C design value of ~1.87 p/m². The piano-riser clear zone stacks on top, so the empty lane runs ~20 m to the riser (qa12-middle-415.6/860/900.png).
- The third-person view shows the avatar alone in a clearing (qa12-third-415.png).
- Mobile (10.9k people) is the same: one sparse row at ~8 m with the stage visible between bodies (qa12-mobile-crowd-860.png).
The blurb promises 'Packed in the pit with the Tribe, 30 m from the stage'. This is the biggest illusion-breaker and it is the very first frame after ENTER.
Evidence: Renders: .shots/qa12-crowd-{100,250,415,710,860,1150,1520}.png, .shots/qa12-middle-{415.6,860,900}.png, .shots/qa12-third-415.png, .shots/qa12-mobile-crowd-860.png. Density numbers came from the in-page probe of layout.pos (bins 3/6/9/12/15/20/30 m, cone cos > 0.5).
Suggested fix: CrowdSystem.ts:103 CLEAR_VIEW = {ring 2.5, cone π/3, r1 6, r2 9, keep 0.35}: change to ring 1.0–1.2, cone ~0.55 rad (±32°), r1 2.5–3, r2 5, keep 0.7. Inside the cone, pick people with height < 1.72 m (hash-select shorter people or scale P.h by 0.94) so the deck line stays visible over the heads. Rely on the existing near-lens dissolve and the player push for anything closer. Do not let the 'piano' clear zone (CLEAR_VIEW_IDS) add a stage-facing cone: give the riser only its 1.5 m ring. Re-check with the same probe: at 'crowd' the 3–6 m density should be ≥ 3 p/m² and at 'middle' ≥ 1.6 p/m² from 4 m out.

## 3. [major] Crowd barrier line (Z 6) ≠ built stage barrier (Z 3): unfenced 3.7 m lane, 'Front row' spot inside it, people on the stage side at the corners (module crowd, lens: Crowd, performers & immersion )
crowd/layout.ts barrierZ() puts the crowd's front edge at Z ≥ 6.7 (6 + 0.7) across |X| ≤ 46, sloping to −1 at |X| 92. The stage builds its barrier at a straight Z 3 across |X| ≤ 90 (stage/layout.ts barrierZ: 3, Deck.ts barrierRuns()). Result:

2) spot 'front' (0, 5.5), labelled 'Front row: on the barrier', sits in that lane. There is no barrier to lean on and no neighbours left or right; the crowd is 1.2 m behind the viewer, facing them (qa12-front-back-860.png, qa12-front-left-860.png).
3) Because the crowd polyline drops below Z 3 at |X| > ~75, 50 people stand on the stage side of the barrier (e.g. x −88.2 z 0.6, x −89.4 z 1.9, x −85.8 z 0.6). qa12-corner-barrier.png shows bodies beyond and overlapping the barrier run.
Evidence: .shots/qa12-pit-overhead.png (free cam −20,14,22), .shots/qa12-front-left-860.png, .shots/qa12-front-back-860.png, .shots/qa12-corner-barrier.png. Probe result: 50 people with |x| < 90 and z < 3.2; front-row band z 6–7 holds 48 people, z 7–8 holds 354.
Suggested fix: Take the crowd front line from one shared source: export barrierRuns() or the barrierZ/barrierX constants from stage/layout.ts and use them in crowd/layout.ts fullDensity() (z < 3 + 0.45 → 0; |x| > 90 → 0 below the arm barriers). Move spot 'front' to (0, 3.6) so the viewer is pressed against the barrier. Keep the zone-A density of 4.5 p/m² from Z 3.5. Keep security inside the pit (Z < 3).

## 4. [major] Phones and lighters are wiped out by crowd.mood cues: 0 phones in In The Cold and in every fireworks finale (module crowd, lens: Crowd, performers & immersion )
In choreo.ts evaluate(), every active 'mood' cue blends ALL 20 channels towards preset[k] × intensity (w → 1 after 1 s). Presets without a PHONES or LIGHTERS key (jump, handsup, sway, hug, fistpump, clap, …) drive those channels to 0 and overwrite the built-in timeline that carries them. The show has cue coverage almost end to end. Sweeping choreo.evaluate over the whole show gave: PHONES > 0.05 in only 248 of 1581 s, mean share 0.044. Samples: t60 0, t176 0, t300 0, t1480 0 (the timeline has PHONES 0.6 from 1473.85, but the cue 'sway' overrides it), t1520 0, t1545 0. Phones exist only inside 'lighters' and 'sit' cues. The design bible §9.4 asks for 'Winter 35 % phones up', 'In The Cold: hands up, phones up (35 %)' and 'phone filming 10 % (35 % during fireworks)'. In the pit at t=1480 (qa12-pit-1480.png) not one phone is visible. The drone and finale shots (qa12-showcam-tribe-1540.png) show a dead-dark crowd under the fireworks.
Evidence: In-page sweep of __app.get('crowd').choreo.evaluate(t) for t = 0…1580: secondsWithPhones = 248, meanPhones = 0.044. Renders .shots/qa12-pit-1480.png and .shots/qa12-showcam-tribe-1540.png. Show cues checked: 1473.85 'sway' 0.7, 1511.02 'jump', 1537.43 'hug'.
Suggested fix: In choreo.ts step 3, blend only the channels the preset defines. Build a per-preset mask when filling PRESET_ARR, as is already done for FLAGS with max(). Then the timeline's PHONES and LIGHTERS survive. Also add a fireworks rule next to the LOOKUP rule: while fireworks cues are active, PHONES = max(PHONES, 0.3).

## 5. [major] Troupe lanterns render as dark or white 'dice' cubes, not glowing lanterns (641–740 s) (module performers, lens: Crowd, performers & immersion )
In the video the bearers' lanterns are the brightest objects in frame. They are flat, pillow-like warm-white panels with strong bloom that light the dancers' faces, arms and costumes from below and in front (v692, 693.5, 697, 704, 707.5, 725, 739.5). Ours are cubes with thick black frames and grey or white faces, with no emission, no bloom and no light on the dancers. In the wider shots they read as floating crates (qa12_troupe3.jpg 693.5, 697, 704; qa12_troupe1.jpg 692, 700). The dancers are lit flat red by the set wash instead of by their lanterns. The mannequin poses (straight arms up, lanterns held away from the body) also differ from the video, where lanterns are held at chest height, cradled, then swung.
Evidence: $ENDSHOW_DATA/work/compare/qa12_troupe1.jpg (648/661/670/681/692/700), qa12_troupe2.jpg (707.5/716/725/729/736/739.5), qa12_troupe3.jpg (693.5/697/704/708.5). Video frames f4/02768, 02774, 02788, 02816, 02830, 02900, 02958.
Suggested fix: Make the lantern mesh a warm emissive panel (practicalLantern #FFF0C0, HDR intensity ~6–10 so it crosses the bloom threshold) with a thin frame, about 0.28 × 0.28 × 0.10 m. In the troupe fragment shader add a lantern key term: for each dancer, the lantern's world position → a warm point-light falloff (1/(1+d²·4)) on the front of the body. Use the lantern-at-chest arm pose (setArm ~70°, forearm bent 60–80°) for 646–700 and the procession, and the raised pose only on the canon waves.

## 6. [major] MC close-ups: mic not at the mouth, crowd-style arms-up hype pose, generic outfit; framing 2–3x wider than the edit (module performers, lens: Crowd, performers & immersion )
In every MC close-up of the video the MC holds the mic at his mouth. His other hand points or gives a thumbs-up at the crowd, at chest to shoulder height (v348.5, 352.5, 361, 370, 405.5, 440.75, 446.5, 453, 459.25, 496.5). Ours holds the mic hand at the shoulder or chest, 20–35 cm from the face. The right arm is raised straight overhead (drop 'hype' windows) or swung sideways, so he reads like a crowd member (qa12_mc2.jpg 440.75, 446.5, 459.25, 496.5; qa12_mc1.jpg 348.5, 352.5, 361). The comment in performers.ts ('mic at the mouth (left hand)', setArm(p.armL, 58, −14, 142, 10)) does not achieve it. Wardrobe: the video shows a dark navy button-up shirt, black cap with a white brim, shorts and white sneakers (v411, v500); ours wears a black T-shirt and cap with 3/4 pants. Framing: ours shows him at 30–50 % of the frame height; the edit shows him at 90–120 % (chest-up) in 352.5, 440.75, 446.5, 453 and 496.5. At 500 our MC is lost in milky fog behind a black foreground speaker.
Evidence: $ENDSHOW_DATA/work/compare/qa12_mc1.jpg (348.5/352.5/361/370/405.5/411) and qa12_mc2.jpg (440.75/446.5/453/459.25/496.5/500). Video frames f4/01394, 01410, 01444, 01480, 01622, 01644, 01763, 01786, 01812, 01837, 01986, 02000.
Suggested fix: Re-solve the mic arm so the hand ends at the chin. After setArm, check the hand's world position against the head: |hand − (head + forward 0.06, down 0.08)| < 0.07 m. Raise the right arm overhead only at v409–412 and in the drop windows ≥ 415.4. In close-up windows use a point/thumb pose (shoulder 70–95°, elbow 20–40°, forearm towards the crowd). Wardrobe: navy shirt, white-brim cap, shorts, white sneakers. Camera: narrow the fov of the MC subject shots 348–459 and 496–498 by about 2x (e.g. 36 → 18–22) or move the cameras 2–3 m closer, so the edit's chest-up framing is matched.

## 7. [major] Walking in the crowd: the nearest ring of people is always half-dissolved into a visible screen-door stipple pattern (module crowd, lens: Crowd, performers & immersion )
The player push (shaders.ts ~469–473: everyone within 1.4 m is moved out to ~0.7–1.4 m) puts the nearest bodies exactly inside the near-lens dissolve band (dithered from ~1.3 m, gone at ~0.9 m). In the dense pit (player at 12, 0, 14, 4.6 p/m², t=250) 3–5 people right in front of the lens are rendered as stippled ghosts with a clear pixel pattern on heads and torsos. The same happens with the free or low camera. At 1280×720 without temporal AA the pattern is plainly visible. It breaks the 'pressed into bodies' feeling exactly where it matters.
Evidence: .shots/qa12-walk-pit-first.png (dense pit, t=250) and the zoomed crop of it. .shots/qa12-corner-pit.png shows the same stipple on the nearest person.
Suggested fix: Separate the two radii. Either push people to ≥ 1.35 m ahead of the lens (outside the dissolve) but only in the ±40° view cone, or start the dissolve only below ~0.55 m. Replace the ordered dither with alpha-to-coverage: MSAA 4x is already on (postfx aa msaa4), so set alphaToCoverage on the crowd-fade material. That gives a smooth 4-level fade instead of a stipple pattern. Alternatively use blue-noise dither jittered per frame.
