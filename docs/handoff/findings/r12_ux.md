## 1. [major] Ferris ride: after boarding the view faces 94-134° away from the stage, and the hanger post blocks the centre (module player, lens: UX, onboarding, show controls,)
Spot 'ferris' is sold as 'Ride the wheel: the show from 34 m up', but the seated first-person view does not face the Mainstage. Boarding with E at spot=ferris (show t 560 to 571) leaves the camera 94° off the stage direction. At the top of the wheel (boardWheel(true), seek 710, show t 714, camera y = 33.2 m, during 'Sacred Flame — burning wings') it is 134° off. The frame shows only distant side-stage lights, an empty dark field, the lake and the gondola's vertical hanger post straight through the middle of the picture. A first-time rider sees none of the show unless they drag the view about 90-130° by hand, which is awkward on touch. Cause in code: PlayerController.ts:49 RIDE_TURN_MAX = 0.8 rad caps the seated auto-turn to about 46° from the arrival yaw, and the arrival yaw faces the wheel. boardWheel(instant) skips the 'board' phase, so turnTo is never set.
Evidence: Measured via camera.getWorldDirection against the direction to (0,-6): degOffStage -94 after E (/Users/agvastgoedmedia/Projects/Q-Crashers/.shots/qa12-ferris-e.png) and -134 at the top (/Users/agvastgoedmedia/Projects/Q-Crashers/.shots/qa12-ferris-top.png; also qa12-ferris-board0.png, qa12-ferris-03-riding.png). Official video at 709-714 s (f4 02836-02856) shows the dragon-wing flame climax, which the rider cannot see.
Suggested fix: Turn the rider to face STAGE_FOCUS during the walk/board phases, so rideYaw0 already points at the stage. Alternatively allow the full turn while sitting down (a slow ~1.5 s eased yaw while seated is comfortable), or cut to the seated pose facing the stage. Put the eye on the stage-side bench, 0.35-0.45 m off the hanger axis, so the post is not in the middle of the view. Also set turnTo in boardWheel(instant). Optionally add a small 'Stage ←' hint if the view is more than 60° off the stage while riding.

## 2. [major] Third-person avatar renders as a glowing grey-white mannequin (module player, lens: UX, onboarding, show controls,)
In camera mode 2 the player's avatar is meant to be a black Defqon.1 tee, black shorts, skin, cap and white socks (Avatar.ts header). It reads as a uniformly lit white-grey mannequin with a cold blue halo. It is not tinted by the red stage wash that lights every crowd member around it, and outfit, skin and cap are unreadable. It looks like a debug placeholder in the centre of the frame for the whole time this mode is used. Cause: the self-highlight term totalEmissiveRadiance += uSelf * pow(1.0 - nv, 1.8), with uSelf = (0.8, 0.86, 0.95) × selfRim (CameraRig.ts:476). An exponent of 1.8 on a thin cylindrical body covers almost the entire silhouette.
Evidence: /Users/agvastgoedmedia/Projects/Q-Crashers/.shots/qa12-third-700-crop.png (autostart&play&t=700&camera=third, ultra); /Users/agvastgoedmedia/Projects/Q-Crashers/.shots/qa12-desktop-12-cam-Digit2-7s.png (show t≈716); qa12-free-04-third-zoom.png (t≈431). Code: src/player/Avatar.ts around line 288, src/camera/CameraRig.ts:476.
Suggested fix: Make the self-highlight a thin outline: exponent 4-6, strength about 0.2-0.3, tinted towards env.stageColor rather than cold white. Alternatively show it only while the avatar is occluded by crowd, via a depth test or a second pass. Let the stage rim light and the printed tee carry the look; check that the skin and cap colours stay visible at t=700 and t=1230.

## 3. [major] Positions list clips its distance column at every viewport (module ui, lens: UX, onboarding, show controls,)
In the Positions panel (T), each row's distance meta is pushed past the list's right edge and cut. The row highlight also runs to the panel edge with no right padding. This is the primary teleport panel and it looks broken at every size tested. Cause: .row-btn uses grid-template-columns: auto 1fr auto together with .panel.positions .row-btn small { white-space: nowrap }. A plain 1fr is minmax(auto, 1fr), so the long nowrap blurb sets the min-content width and the row overflows.
Evidence: '85 m' shows as '8' over 'm', and '76 m', '86 m', '82 m' lose the unit, at 1280x720 (/Users/agvastgoedmedia/Projects/Q-Crashers/.shots/qa12-desktop-11-positions.png), 1366x768 (qa12-1366-positions.png), 1920x1080 (qa12-1920-positions.png) and portrait 390x844 (qa12-mport-11-positions.png). CSS: src/ui/styles.css:1650-1705 and :3527.
Suggested fix: .row-btn { grid-template-columns: auto minmax(0, 1fr) auto; } plus .row-btn > span:nth-child(2) { min-width: 0 }. Keep the ellipsis on small, and add white-space: nowrap to .row-btn .meta so '85 m' never wraps.
